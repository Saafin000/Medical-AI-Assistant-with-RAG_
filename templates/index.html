Agent Capabilities
  • Medical Conversation Agent:
    • General health information and conversational assistance
    • Answers common medical questions with context-aware responses
  • Medical RAG Agent:
    • Docling-based document parsing to extract text, tables, and images from PDFs
    • LLM-based semantic chunking with structural boundaries
    • LLM-based query expansion with related medical terms
    • Qdrant hybrid vector database retrieval
    • Input-output guardrails for safe responses
    • Confidence-based handoff to Web Search to prevent hallucinations
  • Web Search Agent:
    • External medical information retrieval from reliable sources
    • Supplements medical knowledge when confidence is low
    • Provides current medical research and updates
  • Clinical Pre-Consultation Agent:
    • Structured patient information collection
    • Adaptive follow-up questions based on patient responses
    • Red-flag detection for emergency escalation
    • Structured clinical pre-consultation report generation
    • Patient review, correction, and confirmation workflow
  • Computer Vision Agents (Human-in-the-loop):
    • Brain Tumor Detection from medical imaging
    • Chest X-ray COVID-19 Classification
    • Skin Lesion Segmentation and analysis
    • Human verification required for all CV results
